Education and Professional Memberships
PHD in Ophthalmology

Dr. Ahmed Sheikh El Arab holds a prestigious PhD in Ophthalmology, showcasing his advanced expertise and dedication to the field.

Consultant Expertise

Dr. El Arab is a renowned consultant in cornea, cataract, and refractive surgeries, as well as retinal, vitreous body, and eyelid surgeries

Professional Memberships

- Member of Royal College of Surgeons, Edinburgh, England - Member of International Council of Ophthalmology, Geneva, Switzerland - Member of European Ophthalmology Society - Member of Egyptian Ophthalmology Society

Consultant Positions

Consultant of cataract, LASIK, and retina surgeries at the General Air Hospital.

Scientific Contributions

Dr. El Arab has contributed to numerous scientific researches published in both local and international medical journals, advancing the field of ophthalmology.

Professional Experience and Contributions
Hospital Experience

Dr. El Arab has worked in these prestigious hospitals, providing top-notch eye care services.

- Kobri El Kobba Military Hospital - Air Force General Hospital - Air Force Specialized Hospital - Cairo Fatimic Hospital - Eye City Center - Egyptian Eye Center - Nour Al-Hayat Eye Center - Mashreq Eye Center - Experts Eye Center

Medical Convoys

He has participated in many medical convoys to treat eye diseases within Egypt, bringing his expertise to those in need.

International Conferences

Dr. El Arab has been a speaker and participant at many international conferences in America and Europe over the past decade, sharing his knowledge and learning from global experts.

Surgical Expertise

With 15 years of experience, Dr. El Arab specializes in performing micro-eye surgeries to correct vision defects with LASIK, femtosmile, intraocular lens implantation, cataract removal, treatment of corneal diseases (including corneal transplantation, corneal ring transplantation, crosslinking for corneal keratoconus treatment), glaucoma operations, repairs of eyelid defects, and squint corrections in both children and adults.
